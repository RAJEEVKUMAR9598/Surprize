Ek Chhoti Si Story…

15 May 2019. Humein tab nahi pata tha ki ek normal beginning itni saari memories mein badal jayegi.

Time ke saath calls aaye, conversations badhi, comfort badha… aur ek aisa connection bana jise distance bhi easily explain nahi kar sakta.

Kabhi hasi, kabhi gussa, kabhi manana, kabhi ek dusre ko miss karna — perfect nahi tha, but real tha.

Humne screens ke through birthdays celebrate kiye, random moments share kiye, aur ek dusre ki everyday life ka part bante gaye.
